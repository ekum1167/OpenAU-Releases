OpenAU

Release notes

Version 0.2.0 · Preview 1 · September 2026

This is an unsigned early preview. Read the Safety & End User Terms and begin with your output volume low.

What's included

  • An ordered chain of installed Audio Unit effects on the current Mac output.
  • Automatic recovery when changing interfaces, connecting AirPods, or waking the Mac.
  • Persistent plug-in chain, bypass state, and supported plug-in state.
  • Native plug-in editor sizing and multi-display window placement.
  • On-demand whole-Mac CPU, memory, and plug-in audio-load measurements.
  • Launch-at-login and restoration preferences.

Known limitations

  • Apple silicon and macOS 14.2 or later only.
  • Unsigned builds require manual approval in macOS and may request permission again after an update.
  • Some protected audio cannot be captured.
  • A faulty in-process AUv2 plug-in can still crash its host.
  • This preview is not intended for live or production-critical use.
