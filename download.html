Before you download

OpenAU is free, but real-time audio software and third-party plug-ins can behave unpredictably.

Start with your output volume low.
Unexpectedly loud output, feedback, distortion, silence, or interruptions may occur—especially during plug-in or audio-device changes. Do not use OpenAU where a failure could cause injury or equipment damage.
Download OpenAU 0.2.0 Unsigned Apple-silicon preview for macOS 14.2 or later. macOS will require an additional confirmation when opening it.

Opening OpenAU for the first time

Because this free build is not notarized by Apple, macOS may block its first launch. You do not need to disable Gatekeeper or any other Mac security feature.

  1. Open the DMG and drag OpenAU into Applications.
  2. Try opening OpenAU once. If macOS blocks it, close the warning.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to Security. Click Open or Open Anyway for OpenAU, then confirm Open Anyway.
  4. Look for the waveform icon in the menu bar. OpenAU does not display a normal window or Dock icon.
  5. Click the waveform and switch Processing on. This is what triggers the Screen & System Audio Recording request. On some macOS versions the category is named Screen Recording.

No Open/Open Anyway button? Move OpenAU to Applications, try opening it again, and return to Privacy & Security within about one hour. Confirm the Mac uses Apple silicon and macOS 14.2 or later. Organization-managed Macs may prevent users from making this exception. See Apple's instructions.
