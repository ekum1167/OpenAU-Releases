Developer's note

Built for a problem I had myself.

Hi everyone, I'm Ekin. I built OpenAU to solve an issue that had followed me for years. As both a developer and a DJ/producer, I wanted one small, dependable app that would let me run my own Audio Unit plug-ins directly on my Mac's output.

My main use is a corrective EQ for my headphones. I also use analysis plug-ins while listening to music on streaming services, so I can check loudness, stereo image, and tonal balance and compare references with my own tracks—without downloading files or setting up a DAW session.

I tried other apps, but they included features I did not need and could become unstable when switching audio devices. OpenAU focuses on one thing: a simple output plug-in chain that can recover from interface changes, AirPods handoffs, sleep, and restarts.

OpenAU is free, and every download is the same full version. I am not building it around feature restrictions or maximizing profit. If it helps your workflow, optional support lets me spend more time maintaining it, fixing bugs, and adding thoughtful improvements.

Sincerely,
Ekin
